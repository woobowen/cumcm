# Problem selection — SCAFFOLD_ONLY

- **Boundary:** compare available problems against team capability/risk without solving them.
- **Inputs:** ingested problem manifests, team constraints, selection rubric.
- **Outputs:** evidence-backed selection decision and rejected options.
- **Forbidden:** popularity voting, leaked solutions, unsupported feasibility claims.
- **Upstream mechanism to evaluate:** auditable multi-criteria selection and human gate.
