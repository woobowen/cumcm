# Robustness — SCAFFOLD_ONLY

- **Boundary:** test sensitivity, uncertainty, stress, failure modes, and assumption violations.
- **Inputs:** validated runs/models, uncertainty plan.
- **Outputs:** robustness/uncertainty results, failure cases, validity limits.
- **Forbidden:** reporting only favorable perturbations, hiding instability, extrapolating beyond evidence.
- **Upstream mechanism to evaluate:** perturbation registries and uncertainty coverage checks.
