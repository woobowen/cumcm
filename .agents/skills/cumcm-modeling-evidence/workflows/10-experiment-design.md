# Experiment design — SCAFFOLD_ONLY

- **Boundary:** preregister questions, factors, metrics, seeds, budgets, stop rules, and analysis.
- **Inputs:** verified models, hypotheses, constraints.
- **Outputs:** experiment manifest, power/coverage rationale, failure plan.
- **Forbidden:** post-hoc metric invention, unbounded runs, hidden manual intervention.
- **Upstream mechanism to evaluate:** reproducible design matrices and stopping controls.
