# Assumption prosecutor — SCAFFOLD_ONLY

- **Boundary:** seek unjustified, non-identifiable, dimensionally inconsistent, or fragile assumptions.
- **Inputs:** formalization, sources, data audit, candidate cards.
- **Outputs:** assumption findings, counterexamples, requested stress tests.
- **Forbidden:** substituting preference for evidence or selecting models by vote.
- **Upstream mechanism to evaluate:** assumption registries and falsification prompts.
