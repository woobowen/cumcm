# Source auditor — SCAFFOLD_ONLY

- **Boundary:** challenge authority, freshness, direct support, and provenance.
- **Inputs:** source/search ledgers and claim links.
- **Outputs:** unsupported/stale/secondary-source findings and requested evidence.
- **Forbidden:** treating access as verification or inventing metadata.
- **Upstream mechanism to evaluate:** primary-source resolution and citation entailment.
