# Phase 004A Postmortem

## What failed

RC1 produced usable requirements, assumptions, a detailed data audit, a baseline and two structurally
different candidate pipelines. It then stopped honestly: the trusted code registry was fixed to the
two bundled synthetic runners and the CLI had no controlled real-case executor. Consequently there
was no admissible way to produce a real Run manifest.

## Post-unlock review

Unlock occurred at `2026-09-04T06:18:44Z`, only after freeze commit `8e7b7ce` was pushed and the
remote SHA matched. Three official `mcm.edu.cn` resources were retained as metadata/hashes only. An
official display described broad methods including visualization, clustering, seasonal forecasting
and heuristic optimization. That does not prove the RC1 portfolio was wrong: it already proposed
seasonal, hierarchical, robust and optimization families, but could not execute them.

## Root-cause classification

The accepted generalizable defect is execution evidence, not problem-domain method choice. The RC2
change therefore adds a controlled case-local Python runner, immutable execution capture and a
separate manifest-sealing step. Problem-specific method names, parameters, dates, item/category
names, quantities and prices were rejected from the Skill.

The unresolved substantive limitations are data identification limits: observed sales can be
stockout-censored, observed price is endogenous, packaged units are ambiguous, and future realized
outcomes are absent. They remain limitations/data priorities rather than invented evidence.
