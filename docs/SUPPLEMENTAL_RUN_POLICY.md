# Supplemental-run policy

Supplemental acquisition is a new, separately frozen tranche. Only an infrastructure-censored slot,
or a harness-censored slot with hash-bound semantic-equivalence PASS, may be allowlisted. Resolved
successes, terminal negatives and unknown slots are prohibited. A prompt, Schema, fixture, package,
scorer, oracle, model, reasoning or transport change requires a new protocol cohort.

Authorization, pre-audit PASS, immutable budget and exact slot allowlist precede every start. Caps
are at most four starts globally and two per authorized slot, with concurrency one; first resolved
outcome, elapsed/input cap, infrastructure recurrence, drift or any hard Gate stops acquisition.
Missing evidence means zero starts. R1 authorizes zero slots and records zero real starts.
