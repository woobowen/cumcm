# Quality versus reliability evidence

Quality evidence answers whether comparable oracle-passing outputs cover the frozen balanced-case
and independent-repeat design. Reliability evidence describes what happened to every attempt:
completion, eligibility, terminal failures, infrastructure censoring, retries, elapsed time and
tokens. Neither scope substitutes for the other.

R1 quality is insufficient at two of four balanced cases and depth one of two. Reliability is
sufficient only for descriptive reporting of the observed 28-attempt cohort. Its accepted scope is
`RELIABILITY_ONLY`; it cannot establish quality, performance superiority, architecture selection,
base selection or Phase 003 readiness.
