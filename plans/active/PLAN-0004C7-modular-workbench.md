maps M01–M14 to existing artifacts and states. A thin module CLI prepares a scoped request,
validates actual Codex-authored artifacts or invokes the existing numerical/controller path,

execution; distinguish absent identity from malformed explicit values; content-bind supplied
hashes; preserve ordered data bytes; never derive identity from Final. New declarations bind
requirements, assumptions/constraints and relevant frozen experiment semantics. Historical
subjects remain replayable at their own commits, never rewritten to satisfy new expectations.

negative results and review questions. Feedback never supplies executable commands, formal
